Application

Four steps to bone dry

No primers, no special tools. Brush it on, let it cure, stay dry.

1

Clean

Wipe the surface down. Make sure seams and fabric are dry and free of dust and grease.

2

Apply

Brush a generous, even coat directly over seams, holes and the weave you want sealed.

3

Cure

Leave to dry fully. The coating sets into a flexible, UV-stable waterproof membrane.

4

Done
